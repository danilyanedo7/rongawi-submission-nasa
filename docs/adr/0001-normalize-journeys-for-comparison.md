# Play each bird from departure to return

Each bird's recorded journey, from the day it leaves the colony to the day it returns, is played over the same 26 seconds, followed by 4 seconds at home. This makes the three birds easy to compare: each journey keeps its own order of events, and no bird starts with a long silent stretch while waiting for its departure date. The real dates stay visible on the timeline and in the story text.

**Option we did not choose:** one shared calendar clock for all three birds. It would show who was flying on the same day, but Eric leaves on 22 October and Sanne on 11 November, so Sanne would sit silent for the first part of the clip and the three tracks would be harder to compare.
